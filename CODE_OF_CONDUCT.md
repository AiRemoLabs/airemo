# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Airemo to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone who takes part, whatever their
background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer has closed it
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may warn, mute or
ban anyone who breaks these rules. Decisions are made by the maintainer and are final.

## Reporting

Email **airemo.connect@gmail.com** with the subject `CONDUCT`, with links or screenshots if You can.
Reports are kept private, and the people involved are treated fairly.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and anywhere You represent the
project in public.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
