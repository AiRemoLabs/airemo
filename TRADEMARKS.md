# Airemo trademark policy

The Airemo source code is open source (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`NOTICE`](NOTICE)). The
**Airemo name and logo are not**. The licenses give you the code, not the brand: Apache License 2.0
(section 6) and the relay's Functional Source License both say they grant no trademark rights.

This policy explains what you can and cannot do with the name and logo, so that people can always
tell the official Airemo from a modified copy.

"Airemo" and the Airemo logo (the "Prompt Signal" mark: a terminal prompt sending out a signal) are
trademarks of Devakrishnan Gopal.

## You may, without asking

- **Say what your thing works with:** "works with Airemo", "a plugin for Airemo", "Airemo agent
  plugin for Aider".
- **Talk about Airemo:** in articles, videos, talks, reviews, tutorials and comparisons, as long as
  it is clear you are not the Airemo project and are not endorsed by it.
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the name Airemo, for example in a package
  manager, as long as you do not change the code.
- **Use the name in the description of a fork:** "based on Airemo" or "a fork of Airemo".

## You may not, without written permission

- **Name a fork or modified version "Airemo"**, or anything confusingly similar ("Airemo Pro",
  "Airemo Plus", "OpenAiremo", "Ai-Remo"). A modified version must use a different name and logo.
- **Publish an app** in an app store, or a browser extension, under the Airemo name or logo, or with
  a name or icon that looks like it.
- **Offer a hosted service** (a relay, dashboard or anything else) under the Airemo name, or in a way
  that suggests it is the official Airemo service.
- **Use the logo** for anything other than linking to or talking about the official project. Do not
  change it, combine it with your own mark, or use it as your product's icon.
- **Register domains, social accounts or company names** containing "Airemo" that could be mistaken
  for the official project.
- **Suggest endorsement**, partnership or certification that does not exist.

## If you fork

Forking is welcome: that is what the license is for. Please:

1. Choose a new name and a new logo.
2. Replace "Airemo" in user-facing places: the app and command names, window and page titles,
   package names, icons and store listings.
3. Keep the copyright and license notices (the license requires it), and feel free to say
   "based on Airemo".

Internal identifiers such as the `@airemo/*` package scope or the `airemo://` link scheme in the
source can stay while you develop, but rename them before you publish anything under your own name.

## Questions

Not sure whether your use is fine? Write to airemo.connect@gmail.com before you publish. Most
reasonable uses get a quick yes.

This policy may be updated. It does not limit rights you have under trademark law, such as fair
use or nominative use.
